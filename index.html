What it does

Everything you need to run many accounts at once

Built for power users who work across a lot of instances — multi-account games, testing, automation.

Multi-instance mirroring

Every app runs in its own tab on its own isolated virtual display. Switch instantly — no window juggling, no cross-talk between instances.

isolated displays · instant switch

Multi-device

Connect and mirror several Android devices at once, over USB or Wi-Fi, all from one window.

parallel devices

Zones & game controls

Bind any key or mouse button to a tap position — tap or hold — plus a virtual joystick and a mouse-look camera to aim like on PC.

keys · joystick · aim

Broadcast

Fire every tap — and every drag — to all instances of the same app at once. Built for repetitive multi-account work.

one-to-many input
NEW

Lossless per-device audio

Each device streams its own uncompressed sound to your PC — auto-levelled, never clipping — with a per-device mixer. Android 13+

lossless · auto-levelled
NEW

Recording & screenshots

Record the active instance to MP4 or grab a screenshot — device pixels only, no black bars, no cursor.

F9 record · F8 snap

Routines

Record a sequence of taps once and replay it on exactly the instances you choose, at exactly the same pace.

record & replay

Secure Folder

Mirror apps inside Samsung Secure Folder — no root, no workarounds. Keep accounts truly separate.

no root required

Quality presets

Ultra → Eco, or fully custom resolution, FPS, bitrate and DPI.

Auto-reconnect

Loose cable or Wi-Fi blip? The instance comes back on its own.

Screen-off mirror

Keep the phone panel dark while you mirror to save battery.

Clipboard sync

Copy on the device, paste on the PC — and Ctrl+V types your PC clipboard into any field.

Getting started

From plugged-in to piloting in four steps

Watch the guided tour — the same walkthrough the app gives you the first time you open it.

Watch the guided tour

Connect a device

Enable USB debugging, plug in over USB or pair over Wi-Fi — a guided tutorial walks you through it the first time. Nothing to install on the phone.

Spin up instances

Pick apps across every connected device, choose a quality and open them. Each lands in its own tab on its own virtual display.

Wire your controls

Map zones and game controls, set up broadcast and routines, balance audio per device, and save layouts you can restore in one click.

Run the fleet

Switch, control, record and automate a dozen instances from one window — without ever leaving it.
